# Discovery games: original CC0 content, revision 1

The word categories, face words, rhyme-family selections, controlled sentence
vocabulary, explanatory text and workshop presets in src/discovery.rs and
frontend/lessons/discovery-labs.js are original project material dedicated to
CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The Rust/JavaScript engine code otherwise retains the repository's MIT license.
No external dictionary, recorded voice, scraped quotation, AI call or paid asset
is required. General US English is the declared pronunciation convention.

Rhyme families specify final stressed-vowel sounds. They are not a universal
accent model. Word sorting uses explicit everyday category meanings. Sentence
assembly is a controlled five-tile grammar with three accepted adverb positions;
it is not a general grammar checker. Corpus and algorithm revision 1 are fixed
for seeded replay. Introduce versioned generation before changing existing output.

Public faces, seeds and reveal endpoints support learning, not hidden-state
competitive security. The native app and The Lost Lantern are separate work.
